Hi! I'm a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, advised by [Vivek Sarkar](https://vsarkar.cc.gatech.edu/) in the [Habanero Extreme Scale Software Research Laboratory](https://habanero.cc.gatech.edu/).
I'm also a year-round intern at [Sandia National Laboratories](https://proof.sandia.gov/), where I'm working with [Zach Sullivan](https://www.zachsully.com) on the formalization of asynchronous statecharts in the Q Framework.
I'm interested in formal methods and verification, and I'm specifically focused on formal models of concurrency and what they let us prove easily about languages and programs.

Before I started at Georgia Tech, I did my master's at Colorado State University with [Louis-Noël Pouchet](https://www.cs.colostate.edu/~pouchet/), where I helped develop PEQC, an equivalence checker for parallel programs, and [PEQC-MLIR](https://github.com/axolotls73/PEQC-MLIR), which adds an MLIR frontend to PEQC with conversion passes for AMD's AIR and AIE dialects.

On the side, I also like term rewriting and parsing, and if I'm not writing proofs or code, I'm probably out birding (one day I'll see all the warblers in Atlanta!).
